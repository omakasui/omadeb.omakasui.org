# Why Omadeb?

Omadeb was born from the same philosophy that drives [Omakub](https://omakub.org) and [Omarchy](https://omarchy.org): the idea that a developer's environment should be thoughtfully crafted, consistent, and ready to use from day one. But Omadeb is not a fork, a migration, or a successor to either of them. It's a parallel project, not a child.

While [Omabuntu](https://omabuntu.omakasui.org) builds on top of Omakub and extends it for Ubuntu users, Omadeb starts fresh on [Debian](https://debian.org) with [GNOME](https://gnome.org), taking inspiration from both projects but building its own path. The choice of Debian is intentional: a rock-solid, independent base that many developers and system administrators already know and trust.

## Inspired, Not Derived

Omakub showed that a curated, opinionated Linux setup could feel cohesive and welcoming without sacrificing power. Omarchy pushed that aesthetic even further. Omadeb borrows from both in spirit — the curated toolset, the unified themes, the sensible defaults — but implements everything fresh for a Debian and GNOME environment.

This means Omadeb is not constrained by Ubuntu-specific tooling or Omakub's structure. It's free to evolve on its own terms, shaped by the needs and preferences of the Debian ecosystem.

## A Home for Debian Developers

Omadeb's goal is simple: bring that same feeling of a well-configured, beautiful, and productive desktop to Debian. Not because existing solutions are bad, but because that experience deserves to live here too.

If you value stability, simplicity, and good taste in your development environment, Omadeb was built for you.
